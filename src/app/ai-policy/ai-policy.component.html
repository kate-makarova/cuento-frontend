AI Policy

Weekly Game Digest

This site uses an AI system to automatically generate a weekly digest of in-game activity. The digest is compiled from posts written in episode topics only — it does not access private messages, user profiles, or any non-game areas of the forum.

Each episode post is processed individually to produce a short summary. Only posts in designated episode topics are included; all other topics and subforums are excluded from processing.

AI Clarification Messages

When the AI cannot determine certain details from a post on its own — for example, when a character name has not been provided in the digest language — it may send a clarification message to a designated administrator through the site's internal AI chat.

Answers provided in response to these messages are stored in the site's database and may be reused in future digest generations to avoid asking the same question again.

Data Use

Information processed by the AI — including post summaries, clarification notes, and the generated digests — is stored in the site's database. This data is used solely to produce and improve the weekly digest and is not shared with any third parties, except for the AI service provider used to generate the text.
